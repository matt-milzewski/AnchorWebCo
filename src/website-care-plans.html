  • No unlimited edits

Scope rules

Small plans need clear boundaries.

What counts as a minor edit?

A text, link, image or business-detail change on an existing page that fits within the quarterly 15-minute allowance. Unused time expires each quarter and does not roll over.

What happens after an alert?

I confirm whether the issue is real, check the hosting or deployment state and tell you the next action. Repairs outside the plan are quoted or billed at $165/hour with approval.

Can you care for a site you did not build?

Usually, after a paid technical review confirms the hosting, platform and access are supportable. Remediation may be required first.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing date. Annual plans are discounted and run for the prepaid term.
