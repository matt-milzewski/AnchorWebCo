The website challenge

Turn a broad exterior-cleaning offer into clear customer choices.

Exterior cleaning covers visibly different jobs. A customer comparing driveway cleaning is looking at another surface, concern and result from someone enquiring about house washing, roof and gutter work, solar panels, a deck or a commercial exterior. The website needed to present these services without burying them in a long paragraph.

It also needed to establish practical trust for work at a customer’s property. The live site presents the business as family-run and locally owned, states its service coverage and insurance, and gives real before-and-after work a prominent place.

Services presented

  • Driveway and path cleaning
  • House washing
  • Roof, gutter and fascia cleaning
  • Solar panel cleaning
  • Deck and patio cleaning
  • Commercial exterior cleaning

Information architecture

Keep navigation lean, then add detail at the service level.

The live navigation focuses on Home, About, Services, Before and After, and Contact. The homepage introduces individual service categories so a visitor can move from a broad need to the right information. This structure keeps top-level choices manageable while still making specific work discoverable.

  1. Home
  2. About Us
  3. Services
  4. Individual Service Details
  5. Before and After
  6. Contact and Free Quote

The structure is not presented as a universal template. A commercial cleaning company, bond cleaner or multi-service residential operator would need different pages and enquiry questions.

Proof and mobile use

Let the result support the explanation.

Exterior cleaning is naturally visual. The before-and-after section gives customers a direct way to judge the type of work rather than relying only on adjectives. Images use descriptive text and sit alongside the relevant service context.

Mobile visitors can call or request a free quote from the header and action areas. The main service options are presented as clear touch targets, and the page keeps the next step visible after proof and trust information.

What was delivered

A focused service and enquiry system.

  • Responsive, mobile-first page layouts
  • Service categories connected to customer needs
  • Before-and-after proof presentation
  • Business, coverage and insurance context
  • Direct call and quote actions
  • Clear content hierarchy and navigation
