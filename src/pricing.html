What is not hidden in the price

Clear ownership and recurring costs.

Domain and paid software

Your domain is registered in your name. Paid fonts, specialist software, booking tools, email platforms and other third-party services are billed directly to you or listed separately.

Hosting and care

Every new Anchor-built website is paired with a hosting and care plan. The selected monthly or annual plan begins when the website launches and appears alongside the build in your proposal.

What you own

Once invoices are paid, you own the approved business content and the custom site files created for your project. Third-party assets, licensed software and Anchor’s reusable internal tooling remain subject to their existing licences.

If you leave

Monthly care can be cancelled before the next billing date. A current static site export can be provided after outstanding invoices are paid. Transfer to compatible hosting and any migration work are quoted separately.

Revisions

Each proposal states its included review rounds. New functionality or direction outside the agreed brief is priced before it is added.

All prices are in Australian dollars. GST is added only if legally applicable and is shown on the proposal before you accept it.
