About · Anchor Web Co.

A small web studio with a clear local focus.

I’m Matt Milzewski, the designer and developer behind Anchor Web Co. From my Red Hill studio, I lead every project and stay accountable for the strategy, build quality and launch.

Tell me about your project

Local access, direct service

The person you speak with leads the work.

From the first conversation to launch, I lead the planning, design and development. Where a project benefits from specialist support, I remain your point of contact and complete the final technical and launch review.

Being based in Red Hill also makes it easy to understand the local context, meet nearby when useful and stay accessible throughout the project.

Technical background

Business websites backed by real software experience.

My background combines formal software study with enterprise systems work and hands-on delivery for small businesses. That matters when the site needs to stay fast, measurable and maintainable after launch—not just look polished on approval day.

  • Bachelor of Information Technology, QUT
  • Diploma of Software Development
  • More than five years building systems at Telstra
  • Hands-on web design, cloud hosting and technical SEO

Working principles

How projects are handled.

Direct accountability

Matt leads the project and stays your point of contact, so decisions keep their context and questions get practical answers.

Fixed scope before build

Pages, features, review rounds, responsibilities and price are written down before development starts.

Useful over impressive

Design choices need to help visitors understand, trust and act—not merely add motion or decoration.

Search built into structure

Metadata and schema matter, but strong local SEO starts with useful pages and an honest view of the service area.

Maintainable technology

Static delivery and carefully selected integrations reduce the update load, attack surface and performance drag.

Plain-English advice

You will know what is included, what can be measured and where uncertainty remains—without inflated ranking or lead promises.
