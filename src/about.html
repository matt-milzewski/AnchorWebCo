About · Anchor Web Co.

A small web studio with a clear local focus.

I’m Matt Milzewski, the designer and developer behind Anchor Web Co. I work directly with business owners from Red Hill, without handing strategy, design or implementation between departments.

Tell me about your project

Why the inner west

Work closer to the businesses I understand.

Red Hill sits beside a dense mix of independent shops, professional practices, hospitality, trades and home-service businesses. The rebrand gives Anchor Web Co a sharper job: help those nearby businesses present themselves clearly and compete for relevant local searches.

The aim is not to look like a large agency. It is to be responsive, technically capable and easy to deal with.

Working principles

How projects are handled.

Direct communication

The person on the call is the person doing the work, so decisions keep their context and questions get practical answers.

Fixed scope before build

Pages, features, review rounds, responsibilities and price are written down before development starts.

Useful over impressive

Design choices need to help visitors understand, trust and act—not merely add motion or decoration.

Search built into structure

Metadata and schema matter, but strong local SEO starts with useful pages and an honest view of the service area.

Maintainable technology

Static delivery and carefully selected integrations reduce the update load, attack surface and performance drag.

No inflated promises

Rankings and lead volumes cannot be guaranteed. The commitment is to sound implementation, clear reporting and scope you can inspect.
