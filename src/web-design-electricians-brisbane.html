Service architecture

Do not ask one homepage to rank for every electrical service.

A general list of electrical work can confirm broad capability, but it rarely answers the questions behind valuable services. Dedicated pages give switchboard upgrades, smoke alarms, air conditioning, EV chargers, data and communications, emergency work and commercial contracting room for the right details. The goal is not a large page count. It is a clear match between the search, the service and the next action.

Residential electrical

Explain common household work, occupied-home expectations, safety considerations, access, service coverage and whether the customer should call or request a quote.

Commercial electrical

Speak to business owners, builders, facility contacts and property managers. Cover project types, operating-hour constraints, documentation and maintenance capability where accurate.

Switchboards and safety

Use plain language to explain when an assessment may be useful, what the visit involves and why an electrician must confirm the appropriate solution on site.

Smoke alarms and compliance

Keep requirements accurate and current. Separate owner-occupier, rental or property-management pathways if the business genuinely serves them.

EV chargers and air conditioning

These planned services benefit from their own qualification steps, including property type, equipment, electrical capacity, preferred timing and site photos where useful.

Emergency and after-hours work

State availability precisely. If the business does not operate around the clock, the website must not imply that it does. Give urgent visitors the correct phone path and safety guidance.

Trust before contact

Put electrical credentials where customers make decisions.

Licence and insurance information should be easy to find and written accurately. Add genuine Google reviews, an owner or team introduction, clear business details and photographs of completed work with permission. Project captions can identify the type of work and property without exposing an address or claiming an outcome that was not measured.

Residential visitors often want reassurance about punctuality, care and communication. Commercial visitors may look for capacity, relevant project types, safety systems and the ability to work around operations. The website can support both without blending them into vague claims.

Mobile customer journey

Match the action to the urgency.

A customer with a fault may arrive from Google on a phone and need a prominent call action. Someone planning an EV charger or commercial fit-out may prefer to read, compare proof and submit details. The page should keep both routes clear without presenting every visitor with a long form.

Use readable buttons, large tap targets, properly labelled fields and a visible confirmation state. Keep call tracking and form tracking consistent with the rest of the site so useful enquiries can be measured.

Local search foundations

Build relevance around real electrical services and a real coverage area.

Use accurate business details, a well-managed Google Business Profile, consistent service information, useful service pages, crawlable links, unique metadata, a sitemap and appropriate Service and LocalBusiness data. Areas served should reflect where the business actually works. A useful Brisbane-wide service page can mention genuine coverage without publishing near-identical electrician pages for every suburb.

Electrician website FAQs

Straight answers before you plan the build.

How many service pages should an electrician have?

Enough to explain commercially important services with distinct intent. Start with the services customers actively choose and the business wants to sell, then add depth when there is useful information and evidence.

Should emergency electrical work have its own page?

Yes when it is a genuine, reliably available service. State operating hours and coverage accurately, keep the phone action prominent and avoid implying round-the-clock availability if it is not provided.

Can residential and commercial work share one website?

Yes. Separate pathways help each audience find relevant services, proof and enquiry fields while keeping one accurate business identity and domain.

Does the build include electrician SEO?

The build includes technical and on-page foundations such as structure, metadata, internal links, schema and sitemap work. Competitive visibility also depends on reviews, reputation, proximity, competition and ongoing activity.
